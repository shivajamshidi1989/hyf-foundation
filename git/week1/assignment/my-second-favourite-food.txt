Kabab Koobideh

Ingredients:
- 500 g ground beef or lamb
- 1 large onion
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- Tomatoes
- Rice or bread for serving

Instructions:
1. Grate the onion and remove the extra water.
2. Mix the ground meat with the onion, salt, pepper, and turmeric.
3. Knead the mixture well for a few minutes.
4. Put the meat on skewers and shape it evenly.
5. Grill the kebabs over high heat until they are cooked on both sides.
6. Grill some tomatoes next to the kebabs.
7. Serve Kabab Koobideh with rice or bread and grilled tomatoes.
