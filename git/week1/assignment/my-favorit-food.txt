Ghormeh Sabzi

Ingredients:
- 500 g beef or lamb
- 1 onion
- 1 can kidney beans
- 500 g mixed herbs such as parsley, coriander, and chives
- 3 dried limes
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- Salt and pepper
- Oil
- Water

Instructions:
1. Chop the onion and fry it in some oil.
2. Add the meat and turmeric, and cook for a few minutes.
3. Add the herbs and fry them well.
4. Add the kidney beans and dried limes.
5. Add enough water to cover the ingredients.
6. Let the stew cook slowly for about 2 to 3 hours.
7. Add salt and pepper to taste.
8. Serve Ghormeh Sabzi with rice.
